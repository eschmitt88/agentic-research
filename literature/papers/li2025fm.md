# The FM Agent

## TL;DR

Baidu's FM Agent runs autonomous research across ML, operations
research, GPU kernel optimization, and mathematics with a common
recipe: expert-guidance initialization, evolutionary sampling over
proposals, domain-specific evaluators, distributed execution. 43.56%
on MLE-bench, 1976.3 on ALE-Bench, ~20x speedups on KernelBench.

## Claims

- A single agent architecture can succeed across domains with swapped
  evaluators — the ML-specific machinery is less critical than the
  generic search + evaluation loop.
- Evolutionary sampling (keep strong proposals, mutate, discard
  weak) materially outperforms flat sampling for autonomous research.
- Distributed infrastructure with many parallel workers is necessary,
  not optional, to compete at the top of MLE-bench.
- Domain-specific evaluators are the integration point — generic
  agents with per-domain evaluators beat per-domain agents.

## Methods

- Expert guidance initialization: seed the proposal space with
  domain-expert-written prompts or starting solutions.
- Evolutionary sampling loop: population of candidate proposals,
  fitness via domain evaluator, selection + mutation per generation.
- Domain evaluators: MLE-bench, ALE-Bench, KernelBench, math problem
  sets — each provides a deterministic score signal.
- Distributed execution across many workers; explicit budget ceilings
  on wall-time / cost.

## Results

- MLE-bench: 43.56% medal rate.
- ALE-Bench: 1976.3.
- KernelBench: up to 20x speedups over baseline kernels.
- State-of-the-art on several classical math problems with no human
  tuning.

## Critique / open questions

- Headline numbers are strong but the paper spans four domains —
  depth of ablation within any single one is unclear from the
  abstract.
- Evolutionary search needs a well-shaped fitness landscape; domains
  without a scalar deterministic evaluator may degrade.
- "Without human interpretation or tuning" is a strong claim —
  worth checking whether the expert-guidance initialization
  constitutes tuning under another name.

## Trust signals

- **Credibility:** 4 — FM Agent Team at Baidu AI Cloud (major
  industry lab); arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed; no released
  code located. Strong, broad cross-domain SOTA results from a large
  lab; short on peer review and a public artifact.

## Follow-up

- Deep-read the evolutionary-sampling section for the mutation

- Compare distributed-worker architecture to AIRA_2's async multi-GPU
  workers; the two papers are likely solving the same bottleneck
  differently.
- Extract the budget ceilings (wall, cost) FM Agent uses as a
