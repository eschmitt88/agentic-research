## Autonomous research agents (dominant focus)

1. **ResearchClawBench: A Benchmark for End-to-End Autonomous Scientific Research** — benchmark
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07591 · arXiv v1 May 28 2026, v2 Jun 10 2026
   - 40 tasks across 10 scientific domains, each grounded in a real published
     paper; related literature + raw data are provided while the **target
     paper is hidden during evaluation**. Expert-curated multimodal rubrics
     decompose target artifacts into weighted criteria. Strongest agent
     (Claude Code) averages only 21.5; named failure modes: experimental
     protocol mismatch, evidence mismatch, missing scientific core.
   - reason: dominant scientific-research-automation front (cf. AutoMedBench,
     AutoResearchBench, PaperBench). The hidden-target-paper design is a
     textbook `hce-evaluation` instantiation (held-out signal, no test
     leakage); failure-mode taxonomy informs `typed-claim-partition` and
     `citation-anchoring`. Top-priority ingest.

2. **Toward Generalist Autonomous Research via Hypothesis-Tree Refinement (Arbor)** — method
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11926 · arXiv Jun 2026
   - Turns autonomous research from a sequence of local attempts into a
     cumulative process carrying strategy, execution, and evidence across
     time via a refined hypothesis tree. Across six real research tasks
     (model training, harness engineering, data synthesis) Arbor achieves the
     best **held-out** result on all six, >2.5x the average relative held-out
     gain of Codex and Claude Code under the same interface/budget.
   - reason: hits `evolutionary-expansion` / `evolutionary-search-grain` (tree
     refinement as the search structure), `structured-world-model` (carrying
     strategy/evidence across time), and `hce-evaluation` (held-out gain as
     the headline metric). Strong second ingest.

3. **EurekAgent: Agent Environment Engineering is All You Need for Autonomous Scientific Discovery** — method/position
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13662 · arXiv Jun 2026
   - Argues the bottleneck for autonomous discovery is shifting from
     prescribing agent *workflows* to designing agent *environments* —
     "environment engineering" that amplifies productive behaviors
     (open-ended exploration, systematic artifact management) and suppresses
     harmful ones.
   - reason: sharp re-framing that maps onto `file-as-bus` and
     `structured-world-model` (the environment/workspace as the locus of
     coordination) and `scripted-tool-pipelines`. A novel architectural lens
     on the dominant front. Third ingest candidate.

4. **Act As a Real Researcher: A Suite of Benchmarks Evaluating Frontier LLMs and Agentic Harnesses in the Research Lifecycle (AARRI-Bench)** — benchmark
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07462 · arXiv Jun 2026
   - Evaluates whether agents can emulate professional-researcher behavior
     across the full research lifecycle, separating frontier-LLM capability
     from agentic-harness contribution.
   - reason: another evaluation yardstick on the dominant front; the
     LLM-vs-harness decomposition is a useful angle for `hybrid-model-backends`
     and `hce-evaluation`. Defer behind the top 3 (benchmark-dense window).

   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13716 · arXiv May 2026
