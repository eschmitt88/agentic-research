## Runtime memory & context-eviction policy

1. **ACM: Agentic Context Management for Long-Horizon Tasks** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23809 · arXiv late July 2026 (in window at v1)
   - Context management as an explicit agentic subsystem for
     long-horizon tasks. Directly on the context-eviction-policy →
     runtime-memory axis; strongest candidate to seed the runtime
     memory concept the MoC split is waiting on.

2. **Addressable Recall Compaction for Long Context-Window Control in AI Agents** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25066 · arXiv late July 2026 (in window at v1)
   - Compaction that keeps evicted content *addressable* — compact
     out of the window but recall by address on demand. Bridges
     context-eviction-policy and selective-memory-retrieval; the
     lossless-eviction angle is absent from the graph.

3. **LLM Agents Are Latent Context Managers (VISTA)** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30005 · arXiv June 30 2026 (out of window, new angle)
   - Runtime "proprioceptive dashboard" + lossless archive: the agent
     sees its own context state and manages it, no training. Claims
     wins over ReAct, deletion, masking, compaction, and Claude Code
     on LOCA-Bench, transfers across four backbones. Ablation-rich;
     the self-managed-vs-deterministic policy-locus contrast with
     semenov2026beyond is exactly the open axis from the 08-03 cycle.

4. **Remember When It Matters: Proactive Memory Agent for Long-Horizon Agents** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08716 · arXiv July 2026 (in window at v1)
   - Selective *intervention* (memory injected only when it matters)
     ablated against passive bank exposure, always-on injection,
     advisor-only, and general retrieval. Direct evidence for
     selective-memory-retrieval's core claim from the runtime side.

## Typed / formal enforcement

5. **AgentLTL: Trace-Verification for Measuring, Enforcing, and Training Procedural Compliance** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02599 · arXiv July 2026 (in window at v1)
   - LTL trace verification used three ways: measure compliance,
     enforce it at runtime, and train against it. A fourth-source
     attestation for typed-enforcement with a formal-methods spine;
     the measure→enforce→train ladder is a new structural claim.

6. **Type-Checked Compliance: Deterministic Guardrails via Lean 4 Theorem Proving** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01483 · arXiv April 2026 (out of window; included for dead-center fit to the typed-enforcement watch item)
   - Compliance rules as Lean 4 types — violations unrepresentable at
     compile time, in a deployed financial-agent setting. The
     strongest "types, not prose" attestation surfaced so far.

7. **ContextCov: Deriving and Enforcing Executable Constraints from Agent Instruction Files** — paper
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00822 · arXiv March 2026 (out of window; included for dead-center fit)
   - Compiles CLAUDE.md-style prose instruction files into executable,
     enforced constraints. External validation of the
     instruction-ablation phase-3 move (prose-programs → scripts);
     joins typed-enforcement to scripted-tool-pipelines.

## Budget & halting

11. **ZEBRA: Zero-shot Budgeted Resource Allocation for LLM Orchestration** — paper
    - https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20485 · arXiv May 2026 (out of window, new angle)
    - An allocation agent estimates per-phase utility curves and
      solves a continuous knapsack (water-filling) over the budget.
      Budget as *allocation problem* vs the graph's budget-as-ceiling
      (halt-only) — the same axis khan2026token's pre-flight
      reservation sits on; relevant to the pending /elevate item.

12. **EcoAgent-Bench: Economic Decision-Making in Budget-Constrained LLM Agents** — paper
    - https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05519 · arXiv August 2026 (in window)
    - Benchmark for evidence-conditioned escalation, model-tier
      routing, and stop-loss decisions under budget. Measurement
      substrate for the budget-as-ceiling / hybrid-model-backends
      pair; the stop-loss framing is halting-adjacent.
