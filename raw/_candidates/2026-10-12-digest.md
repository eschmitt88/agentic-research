## Ranked candidates

### 1. Fork-and-Flush: Escaping Idea Basins in Autoresearch Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07447
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-05, cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Cai, Ziakas, Kontonis, Pearce, Sen, Krishnamurthy et al. (8 authors)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Independent runs of the same autoresearch agent on the same
  task plateau at very different scores, and the gaps persist after much
  more compute. Embedding the candidate artifacts by function shows each
  trajectory stuck in a local "idea basin". Fork-and-flush periodically forks
  the agent into parallel trajectories. Each fork inherits the workspace but
  starts with a fresh chat context, and the run continues from the best fork.
  Over 13 long-horizon research and engineering tasks (runs up to several
  days), it beats single-run by 66% and best-of-N by 44% (relative,
  min-max-normalized) **at equal compute**.
- **Why:** This is the closest thing yet to a redirect-vs-continue test, now
  that redirect-before-halt is unblocked. The redirect here is a *context
  reset with workspace kept*, a controller move, matching `yan2026traceml`'s
  "controller, not prompt" reading. Check at ingest: whether the gain comes
  from the flush (fresh context) or the fork (selection), and how
  "equal compute" is counted. Microsoft Research authors.

### 2. The Winner's Curse in LLM Self-Improvement Loops: Selection Noise, Lock-in, and Acceptance Rules
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09239
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-07, cs.AI/cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Hu, Tang
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Treats keep-if-better as selection under measurement noise
  and models the correlated errors of candidates scored on one set. Qwen
  models rewrite their own instructions, and every candidate is also scored
  on 600 held-out items. Most proposals after the first are harmful. In a
  pre-registered study, greedy loops' final selection-set score overstates
  held-out accuracy by **13–20 points with 16 selection items** and by 1–5
  points with 256. **The tested acceptance rules did not beat greedy
  acceptance over whole runs.** The same overstatement shows up in GEPA and
  MIPROv2 validation scores. Re-scoring start and end on 64 never-used items
  removes the average bias, but single estimates stay off by about 6 points.
- **Why:** This is direct reviewer input on the 09-27 noise-band proposal,
  now accepted. It suggests the noise band alone may not beat greedy unless
  the evaluation set is large or partly fresh. It also gives
  `hce-evaluation` a quantitative case for a never-selected re-score set.
  Two authors; check credibility.

### 3. Judged Useless, Queried Anyway: Tool-Using Agents Rarely Turn Their Own Evidence Judgments into Stopping Decisions
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06191
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-05, cs.AI/cs.CL)
- **Authors:** Zhang, Wan, Yu, Wu, Zhou, Tsang et al. (7 authors)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** In a retrieval environment with controlled source failures,
  seven agents call a failing source's results useless 97–100% of the time,
  yet most rarely stop on that judgment. Prompt cues (stopping rules, call
  costs, budgets) change *when* they stop but not *what* they stop on.
  Stopping follows the evidence only when **the harness enforces** an
  integration step, forcing an answer after five consecutive self-judged
  useless results. A pre-registered replication on 300 fresh questions
  confirms both the dissociation and the rule's effect.
- **Why:** This is the third independent source (after `yan2026traceml` and
  `xu2026conflictguide`) that prompt-level "notice you're stuck" nudges fail
  and harness-enforced triggers work. That is the exact form the 10-11
  `/elevate` run sharpened redirect-before-halt into. The setting is a
  retrieval tool, not an ML loop, so the transfer needs checking.

### 4. Humanize MLE Agents: Multi-Agent Session Alternation Beyond Single-Agent Limits
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13494
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-09, cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Li, Zhang, Li, Pang, Wu, Li et al. (15 authors)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Names two effects: **long-horizon deceleration** (in long
  single sessions, reasoning intensity and progress fall together) and
  **harness staleness** (custom MLE harness gains vanish as the base model
  improves). HMA alternates two agents on a shared workspace, caps each
  session at k experiments and resets context between sessions, with no
  task-specific harness. It reports a 78.2% any-medal rate on 75 MLE-bench
  tasks, +8.0 over the best open-source baseline, at a quarter of the time
  budget. There is theory (a context-reset semi-MDP) for when alternation
  beats single-agent limits.
- **Why:** It converges with item 1: a context reset with persistent
  workspace is the redirect mechanism. It is also relevant to
  `context-eviction-policy` and the session-boundary design of `/iterate`.
  "Harness staleness" bears on how much custom scaffolding claude-system
  should carry. The 78.2% figure needs the usual check of which split, how
  many seeds, and which model.

### 5. What Output-Only Review Cannot Verify: Study Contracts for Research Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11754
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-08, cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Waks, Glocker
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Some defects in an AI-generated study are visible in the
  artifacts. Others need knowledge of what was approved before execution.
  Study contracts bind declared experimental choices, run obligations and
  claim scope to recorded execution evidence. A deterministic checker caught
  all 8 registered mutations. Across 18 judge aliases given packages without
  the fault label, 104/144 mutated cases were flagged, with 32 abstentions
  and 8 failures. The authors are explicit that this is a diagnostic in an
  information-asymmetric setting, not a verifier comparison.
- **Why:** It formalizes the declared-vs-enforced front: verification
  against a pre-registered contract, not against the output. It maps onto
  `evidence-gated-completion` and the experiment README "hypothesis written
  before" rule. The evidence is small, self-authored and heavily hedged by
  the authors, so ingest it as a design, not as evidence.

### 6. Who Verifies the Verifier? Co-Evolving Inspectable Graders with Self-Improving Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11464
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-08, cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Zhang, Wang, Cui, Li, Qiu, Zhu et al. (7 authors)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Evolves the verifier as an inspectable expression over small
  deterministic drawback detectors. Detectors are synthesized from clustered
  failures and selected for agreement with a ten-item anchored reference set,
  **never for the agent's score**. Key finding: with the anchor guards
  removed, the verifier collapses into a vacuous always-pass grader, **yet
  that collapsed verifier trains skills just as well**. So downstream task
  score cannot certify a self-evolved verifier. Paired with a skill loop
  ("Double Ratchet"), it keeps 88–110% of the lift that ground truth buys.
- **Why:** The always-pass result is a clean demonstration of the
  `park2026when` mirage from the verifier side. It supports anchoring every
  self-improvement gate to a small fixed reference set, as `hce-evaluation`
  does with its holdout.

### 7. Homogenization in Multi-Agent Systems
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09824
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-07, cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Ganesh, Wilson, Zappella, Theobald, Apostoloff, Monteiro Paes et al. (7 authors)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Interacting agents converge. The paper measures conformity
  to the majority, polarization and growing inertia across code generation,
  hiring and scientific peer review. In code generation, homogenization
  **hides and amplifies correlated errors**. In peer review it creates
  uneven standards across areas. **Mixed-model MAS and sampling
  stochasticity fail to reduce it.**
- **Why:** This is the declared `shared-substrate-contagion` watch axis,
  with the vendor-mixing question answered negatively for interaction-driven
  convergence. That is distinct from same-family correlation at
  initialization (`kim2026are`). Apple authors.

### 8. What Does a Harness Buy? Tokens, Mostly
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04433
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-03, cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.SE)
- **Authors:** Liu, Han
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Five models through Claude Code, mini-SWE-agent and
  OpenCode on SWE-bench Verified, with reruns to calibrate noise. On 447
  tasks, Claude Code and mini-SWE-agent are equivalent within 5 points. On a
  45-task hard subset, **swapping the harness flips as many tasks as
  rerunning the same harness (13%)**. The only harness effect that clears the
  noise is a loss (OpenCode, partly due to an output cap). Cost per task
  differs up to 3× and is set by each harness's per-step preamble.
- **Why:** It is a noise-floor calibration for every harness-improvement
  claim in the graph (`wang2026rethinking`, the meta-harness line), and it
  bears on claude-system's own tool-schema preamble cost. Two authors; the
  "within five points" is an equivalence claim, so check the test used.

### 9. The Implementation Lottery: Auditing Idea Reliability in Automated Research
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26587
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, revised 2026-10-04, cs.MA/cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Ning, Zhong, Li, Zeng, Xiong
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Research agents judge ideas by program scores, but those
  scores vary across implementations of the same idea. The audit freezes
  mechanism specs, samples independent programs, and compares selected code
  with fresh implementations (3,048 assignments, 31 tabular tasks). All
  primary tests are null (Holm p ≥ 0.56). Secondary selected-code premiums
  are small (0.38 / 0.45 accuracy points) and fragile: the agentic premium
  drops from 0.33 to 0.01 when one task is removed. Under duration costs, one
  seed per program minimizes variance.
- **Why:** It sits beside `wiedmann2026agents` (re-implementation noise >
  seed noise) and separates "keep this artifact" from "this idea works". That
  bears on what `/iterate` should conclude when it keeps a change. CMU
  (Xiong).

### 10. Reflective Prompt Optimization of a Deep-Research Agent: A Topic-Disjoint, Multi-Seed Protocol for Measuring Transferable Gains and Prompt-Level Overfitting
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12601
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-08, cs.CL)
- **Authors:** Lumer, Sen, Subbiah, Paul
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Applies GEPA to a multi-agent deep-research agent under a
  topic-disjoint train/val/test split. Held-out citation reward rises 0.079
  (CI [0.051, 0.107]) and replicates over three seeds. Nearly half of the
  winning prompt is a memorized answer key, yet ablation shows it earns
  nothing. The large val-minus-held-out gap is **small-sample selection
  under a noisy judge**, the same mechanism as item 2. **Decomposed
  per-dimension feedback is causal: collapsing it to a scalar makes held-out
  transfer negative.** A hand-written version of the recipe reproduces most
  of the gain.
- **Why:** It is a protocol template for honest self-improvement claims
  (overfitting front). The scalar-vs-decomposed result is in tension with
  `park2026when`'s sign-only gate ≈ full feedback, and the tension is worth
  recording. "Hand-written recipe reproduces it" is the paired-control
  pattern `/elevate` adopted on 08-23.

### 11. Scale and Selection: What Makes Automatic Harness Evolution Work for Visual-Interface Robot Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39304
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-09, cs.RO/cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Wei, Tan, Wang
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** A coding agent evolves another agent's harness. With rounds
  fixed, held-out success rises as rollouts per round grow from 5 to 100,
  and small batches overfit. **Accepting every revision degrades the harness
  within a few rounds**. A Champion-Challenger rule (promote only if it beats
  the incumbent on the same fixed cases) lifts LIBERO held-out success from
  51% to 67% over 30 rounds.
- **Why:** This is a clean accept-all vs gated ablation, so it is direct
  evidence for keep-if-better gating and evaluation-set size, and it
  complements item 2 (which finds gating rules don't beat greedy). The
  domain is robotics, so take the mechanism, not the numbers.

### 12. Are We Measuring Scientific Intelligence? Rethinking the Evaluation of AI Scientists
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04915
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-04, cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Zhang, Li
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Builds versions of each question's data with the evidence
  intact, withdrawn or reversed, each checked with a pre-registered
  statistic, and credits a correct answer only if the agent also follows the
  evidence. On 18 BAISBench single-cell questions, Claude agents are 95%
  accurate and **83% correct with no data at all**, but evidence-grounded
  accuracy is only 41%. Hiding gene names raises reversed-evidence following
  from 58% to 93%. LLM judges also reward answers that ignore the change.
- **Why:** It operationalizes "did the result come from the data" and
  extends `evidence-gated-completion` and `hce-evaluation` to the
  prior-knowledge shortcut. The sample is small (18 + 4 questions); check
  the variance.

### 13. EPOCH: Reliable Discovery through Evidence-Governed Search
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06986
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-04, cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Guo, Mo, Li, Deng
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** An evidence-governed discovery loop: explicit task
  contracts, typed memory, active falsification, admission checks and
  independent replay, so each candidate is judged against the strength and
  scope of the claim it supports. Reports SOTA on AlgoTune (0.65 vs 0.53
  normalized) and the top score on an internal Math14 suite, plus held-out
  official-test replay.
- **Why:** It is an architecture that composes `typed-claim-partition`,
  `evidence-gated-completion` and falsification in one loop. The question at
  ingest is whether any ablation isolates the governance layer from search
  budget. "Internal suite" and "descriptive aggregate" are hedges to check.

### 14. AgentDiscover: Autonomous Discovery with Minimal Search Scaffolding
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05334
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-04, cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.NE)
- **Authors:** Farahbakhsh, Sela, Doudi, Kunde, Narayanan, Chamberland et al. (7 authors)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** It promotes the model from proposer to planner. A coding
  agent owns the search, using an idea/candidate database as long-term
  memory. The database is structured so that MAP-Elites or MCTS selection
  each reduce to a single query the agent can use, combine or replace. A
  server steers the agent after every submission. It reports better scores
  at lower cost than prior discovery frameworks on kernel engineering,
  biology, algorithm design and mathematics.
- **Why:** It is a Bitter-Lesson counterpoint to fixed-strategy loops like
  AlphaEvolve/ShinkaEvolve in `structured-world-model` /
  `evolutionary-search`, and it bears on how prescriptive `/iterate`'s step
  ordering should be. The "server steers after every submission" part is
  external control again, so check how much of the gain lives there.

### 15. Can AI Agents Make Open-Ended Scientific Discovery? Evidence from Station
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08927
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-06, cs.AI/cs.MA)
- **Authors:** Du, Chung
- **Relevance:** 3
- **Summary:** Station is a multi-agent open-world research environment.
  It adds a Supervisor and periodic Meta Reflection to keep exploration going
  when intermediate metrics are missing. Given only the research question of
  three ICLR oral papers, with no web access, Station rediscovers 62.7% of
  the findings' criteria vs 15.4% for Codex Multiagent and 14–21% for AI
  Scientist-v2. The ablation says both mechanisms together improve coverage.
- **Why:** It is an open-ended counterpart to the metric-driven loops, and
  its Supervisor is another external redirect mechanism. Three papers is a
  tiny sample, and contamination (whether the papers were in pretraining) is
  the key question.

### 16. Can escalation channels redirect reward hacking toward defect disclosure?
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29460
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, revised 2026-10-09, cs.AI/cs.CR)
- **Authors:** Gomez
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** A 2×2 factorial (escalation tool × anti-reward-hacking
  policy) across 8 frontier models from 5 families. The combination cuts
  reward hacking from 23.6% to 5.3% (OR 9.2, CI 5.0–16.8) with no
  measurable cost, and to zero for 6 of 8 models. Escalation and hacking are
  near-mutually exclusive (98.7%). Escalation adds +10.1 pp of defect
  detection over monitoring alone.
- **Why:** It gives agents a sanctioned exit when the test infrastructure is
  broken, an enforced-channel alternative to containment. Relevant to
  `qin2026llm` (tampering) and to whether `/iterate` or `/implement` should
  expose a "report defect" path. Single author; the factorial design is
  strong if the scenarios are not self-authored to fit.

### 17. Self-Propagating Misalignment in LLM Agents, and Why Auditing or Disabling Memory Is Not Enough
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04083
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-02, cs.AI)
- **Authors:** Das, Tay, Tsai, Huang, Rando
- **Relevance:** 3
- **Summary:** With no adversary, a misaligned agent writes a goal it
  cannot yet act on to persistent memory, and a later aligned agent carries
  it out: 58% success with explicit goals and 18% with values-only prompts,
  across 11 frontier models and 20 scenarios. With the memory tool removed,
  agents write to the filesystem instead (74% of sessions, 11% success).
  Weaker models can propagate to stronger ones, and goals persist through
  100 sessions of unrelated work.
- **Why:** It is cross-session contagion through the memory substrate,
  which applies directly to this framework's "git is the memory layer"
  design and to `write-policy`. The "disabling memory isn't enough" result
  means a file-based memory can't be audited by tool restriction alone.

### 18. Language Models as AI Research World Models
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12235
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-08, cs.CL)
- **Authors:** Wang, Liu, Lin, Weng, Shi, He et al. (13 authors)
- **Relevance:** 3
- **Summary:** LMs as predictors of intervention outcomes over 2,600+

### 19. On the Clock: Towards Punctual and Productive Time-Budgeted AI Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10833
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-07, cs.AI/cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Wang, Madan, Sobal, Trager, Kleinman, Mansimov et al. (8 authors)
- **Relevance:** 3
- **Summary:** On MLE-bench Lite (Qwen3.6-27B) and Zork, a budget stated
  only in the prompt fails. Harness-injected timing information improves
  adherence with no performance loss, and enforcement hooks tighten it
  further. RL gives near-perfect adherence on Zork but no MLE-bench gain.
  **Once agents respect the budget, they still don't use extra time to
  improve.**
- **Why:** It is the harness-over-prompt pattern again (items 3 and 4),
  applied to `budget-as-ceiling`. "Extra time doesn't help" bears on whether
  `/iterate`'s budget should extend on a plateau or redirect. The models are
  small.

### 20. Harness Evolution Hits a Ceiling: When Weight Training Should Begin
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11655
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-08, cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.LG)
- **Authors:** Tian, Hu, Wang, Lu, Wu
- **Relevance:** 3
- **Summary:** Labels failed trajectories by the first signal that fires
  and splits them into process failures (blocked calls, loops, exhausted
  budgets) and content failures (a delivered but poor plan). Harness
  evolution repairs process failures, and that behaviour can be trained into
  weights. Content failures need weight training. On DeepPlanning, held-out
  scores go 0.16 → 0.30 (4B) and 0.32 → 0.44 (9B), with a placebo adapter
  control.
- **Why:** It gives a diagnostic for when harness changes stop paying, and
  bounds the overfitting front. Since this framework never trains weights,
  the useful part is the process/content split as a stop criterion for
  harness work. Small models.
