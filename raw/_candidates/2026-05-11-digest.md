## 2. GSAR: Typed Grounding for Hallucination Detection and Recovery in Multi-Agent LLMs

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23366
- **Source:** arXiv (2604.23366)
- **Submitted:** 2026-04-25
- **Authors:** Federico A. Kamelhar
- **Summary:** Partitions claims into a four-way typology (grounded,
  ungrounded, contradicted, complementary) and couples grounding scores to
  a three-tier decision function (proceed, regenerate, replan) for
  multi-agent hallucination detection and recovery.
- **Why included:** Extends `citation-anchoring` from a passive provenance
  field to an active control signal — the three-tier decision function is
  a concrete mechanism for what `hce-evaluation` and `citation-anchoring`
  currently describe abstractly.
- **Touches concepts:** `citation-anchoring`, `hierarchical-delegation`,
  `hce-evaluation`
- **Suggested action:** `/fetch-paper 2604.23366`.

## 3. Rethinking Experience Utilization in Self-Evolving Language Model Agents (ExpWeaver)

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07164
- **Source:** arXiv (2605.07164)
- **Submitted:** 2026-05-08
- **Authors:** Weixiang Zhao, Yingshuo Wang, Yichen Zhang, Yanyan Zhao, Yu Zhang, et al.
- **Summary:** ExpWeaver exposes accumulated experience as an *optional*
  runtime resource rather than applying it at fixed points; argues prior
  work over-focused on how experience is stored vs. when it is used.
- **Why included:** Reframes the read-side of `agent-native-memory` —
  current concept implicitly assumes memory is read on demand, but
  ExpWeaver makes the *policy of reading* an explicit design axis. Also
  intersects `evolutionary-expansion` (the loop's prior cycles are the
  "experience" being utilized).
- **Touches concepts:** `agent-native-memory`, `evolutionary-expansion`
- **Suggested action:** `/fetch-paper 2605.07164`.

## 4. SkillOS: Learning Skill Curation for Self-Evolving Agents

- **Source:** arXiv (2605.06614)
- **Submitted:** 2026-05-07
- **Authors:** Siru Ouyang, Jun Yan, Yanfei Chen, Rujun Han, et al. (Google)
- **Summary:** RL framework that trains a *skill curator* to maintain an

- **Suggested action:** `/fetch-paper 2605.06614`. Read alongside
  `saha2026skilldex` for the architectural comparison.
