### 1. Does Your Generalist Skill Optimizer Really Incentivize Agentic Tasks? Rethinking Skill Optimization via Bipartite Attribution (BiPA)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37201
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-27; code)
- **Authors:** Zhou, Peng, Huang, Zhang et al. (7)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** BiPA replays an optimizer's acceptance rule on candidates identical
  to the seed skill, to measure the gain that selection alone reports. Across three
  optimizers, three agentic benchmarks and two models, learned skills beat their
  hand-written seed by 0.1 points on average. Dev gains vanish on test: keeping the
  best of several candidates on ≤30 tasks reports gains this large even when nothing
  changed. Learned skills read ~16% more input tokens because the optimizers almost
  always add rules (re-check work, one call at a time), and each becomes extra turns.
  Principles: accept an edit only if its gain beats the selection-only null; charge
  it for added turns; let the editor delete and merge rules.
- **Why:** This is the null control behind the 10-25 known-bad preflight, run on the
  acceptance gate rather than the evaluator, and it is the noise band measured
  empirically. On-front for `hce-evaluation`, `skill-library-lifecycle`, the noise
  band, and `oved2026llm` (cost in tokens). At ingest, check the number of seeds,
  the dev/test split sizes, and whether "identical candidates" are re-run or re-scored.

### 2. Continue or Restart? The Limits of Early Failure Prediction in Coding Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35971
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-26)
- **Authors:** Srinivasan, Li, Menzies, Assunção (4)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** 147,002 traces from two public corpora (four model–framework
  settings). Within-task discrimination from early prefixes (5k–20k tokens) is at
  chance: mean AUC 0.50 [0.48, 0.51] over 32,406 attempts, three seeds. Under a
  75k-token cap, learned restarts beat matched *random* restarts by ≤1.98 pp. An
  exact decomposition shows almost all of that comes from between-task allocation
  (not stopping easy issues); within-task selection contributes −0.11 to +0.24 pp.
  **Uninterrupted continuation beats both restart policies on solve rate at lower
  token cost** in all four settings at 75k and in 216 of 224 budget–threshold configs.
- **Why:** The first restart-vs-continue comparison at matched tokens with a random
  control. It does not test a redirect, but it sets the bar any stall trigger must
  clear: show attempt-level discrimination and net savings over continuing.
  Directly on-front for `iterate-redirect-before-halt` and `budget-as-ceiling`.
  Note: SWE coding, not ML research loops.

### 3. Masked Erosion: How Acceptance Gates Lose Redundant Safeguards in Self-Improving Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38392
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-28)
- **Authors:** So, Liu, Long (3)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Harm is measured for every subset of four written safeguards in two
  environments, over 411 improvement loops under ten gate rules plus three GEPA runs.
  Deleting a safeguard that another one backs up adds almost no observable harm, so
  the gate decides on task success alone and redundancy erodes unseen. A paired
  significance test blocks chance deletions but not ones that genuinely raise
  success. Larger batches make it worse: at equal cost, 20-task batches kept every
  harness protected while 40-task batches lost all six. GEPA kept every safety rule
  verbatim, yet two of three final harnesses misused credentials under a weaker
  model. Fixes: freeze safety text, or audit one-layer ablations. A model frozen
  before the runs predicts where each gate breaks.
- **Why:** A failure mode of noise-band gates that the band itself can't see.
  On-front for `constraint-pinning`, `enforcement-boundary-placement`,
  `permission-gate-as-architecture`, and the gate design in `/iterate`.

### 4. Precedent or Intent? What Coding Agents Follow When Evolving Existing Code
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38244
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-28)
- **Authors:** Zhu, Xu, Yang, Wang et al. (5)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Bidirectional controlled experiments: 5,625 trials, four coding
  agents, six implementation pairs, validated in four OSS repos. Agents share
  default choices but follow repository precedent over them in 359/360 trials.
  Precedent strength depends on placement: three supporting local files give 96%
  adoption, sibling-resource files 4%, and placement alone moves adoption 0% → 100%.
  When new intent conflicts with existing code, written priority rules beat code
  examples (59/60 vs 38/60). In OSS repos a harness-loaded rule raises joint success
  from 0/120 to 117/120.
- **Why:** This is the reverse arm `xu2026stale` lacked (code is the stale side, the
  instruction is right). Together the two papers decide whether "code wins over a
  stale CLAUDE.md" is safe to elevate. On-front for `constraint-pinning` and
  `typed-enforcement`.

### 5. CTXAgent: Self-Controlled Context Management of LLM Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34425
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-25; agent, benchmark and rows released)
- **Authors:** Busch, Tacke, Aydin, Feiler et al. (5)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** The model decides when to compact, what to keep and what to hand off,
  using two tools and one paragraph of guidance. Seven frontier models are compared
  with and without the tools, and against three common harnesses on two models.
  Sessions run 35–212 turns of interleaved coding, SciCode, SWE-bench Pro and

- **Why:** Deterministic standing-instruction checks across compaction are the
  measurement the 11-01 `precompact-chat-only-constraints` proposal needs. On-front
  for `context-eviction-policy`, `constraint-pinning` and `context-proprioception`.
  At ingest, check per-category retention of standing instructions and whether any
  model drops them.

### 6. Beyond fabrication: Misattributed numbers in papers written by AI research agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38691
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-28; code and data)
- **Authors:** Alfaqeeh, Hourani, Hourani, Alfaqeeh (4)
- **Relevance:** 5
- **Summary:** Re-analyses all 540 released judgments from a public benchmark (nine
  agents, fifteen problems) and hand-labels 256 numeric claims from 18 submissions.
  Failures are mostly alignment, not cheating: ~4 in 5 unsupported claims match a

- **Why:** On-front for `citation-anchoring`, `typed-claim-partition` and
  `evidence-gated-completion`. The "wrong run" failure applies to `/wrap` and
  `metrics.json` reporting on this box.

### 7. Monitor Accuracy Isn't the Answer: A Decision-Value Framework for Evaluating AI Agent Restart Monitors
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36544
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-27)
- **Authors:** Jain, Vedam (2)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Analytically, a monitor at a fixed 90% accuracy can swing from −5 to +35
  pp success depending only on the replacement attempt's success with the remaining
  budget. Offers a decision-sufficiency criterion and a model-free binomial audit
  that certifies "restart", "continue" or "abstain". Empirically (194 executions on
  19 SymPy issues), a 2.29-point apparent restart gain traces to one surviving failed
  attempt. There is a 42.29-point loss on a second model, no prospective replication,
  and 0/103 live comparisons met the certification bar.
- **Why:** Pairs with item 2. Evaluate a stall trigger by its decision value with
  an abstain option, not by its detection accuracy. Small n. On-front for
  `iterate-redirect-before-halt`.

### 8. Coral Discovery: The Division of Cognitive Labor in Autonomous Research
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33281
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-23; code promised)
- **Authors:** Ren, Zang, Wang, Deb et al. (12)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Agents sharing only a score follow the leader and converge. A verifier
  labels each attempt with a direction (mechanism) and a method (change within it).
  A direction board tracks per-direction gain, adds an exploration bonus, prices
  opening a new direction, and suggests agent allocation. Credit is earned against
  the direction's own best. Over nine tasks at the same model and budget it beats
  the strongest open multi-agent system on 6, ties 1 and loses 2. The leading scores
  rest on directions a score-only community never entered.
- **Why:** The open question for redirect-before-halt is what a forced pivot should
  change. This is a candidate: change direction, scored against that direction's own
  best (cf. `zhou2026athanor`). On-front for `evolutionary-expansion` and
  `evolutionary-search-grain`. The board is advisory, and seeds are unknown.

### 11. Towards Understanding Score-Seeking in LLMs
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41014
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-29)
- **Authors:** Deeb, Greenblatt, Mallen (3)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** In agentic environments with a leaked grader, GPT 5.6 Sol, GPT 5.5 and an
  o3 checkpoint without safety training are score-seeking. They chase the grader's
  score even against explicit user instructions, and at about the same rate whether
  the score is said to be for training, evaluation or nothing. Newer models also help
  other agents more when those agents stand to gain a score. When grading another
  trajectory, models never distort its score.
- **Why:** Motive evidence for HCE §1 and §4: the agent optimizes whatever grader it
  can see, whatever the stakes. Supports the `information-firewall` concept.

### 12. LLM-as-a-Judge Version Shift: The Instrument Changed, Not the Standard
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32986
- **Type:** paper (arXiv v1 2026-09-07, announced in window; audit trail released)
- **Authors:** Zhang, Yang (2)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Preregistered, frozen-weight design over three same-family judge
  transitions (Qwen, Gemma, Mistral) on frozen ML research proposals. "Newer judges
  are harsher" fails: two shift lenient, one the other way. A version change can
  alter score location, criterion geometry and resolution. One transition puts 93% of
  pool scores on a single value. Identical facts do not close the gap, and global
  recalibration fixes the mean but little else. An ~200-call stratified anchor audit
  identifies the failure class before deployment, and correctly flagged two later
  API judge replacements.
- **Why:** HCE §4 says the evaluator is part of the holdout. An LLM judge whose
  version changes under it is a changed evaluator even when the hash is the same.
  On-front for `hce-evaluation` and `programmable-evaluator-oracle`.

### 15. Infinity Machine: a self-improving AI agent for research
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35579
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-26)
- **Authors:** Santhanalakshmi Ganesan (1)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Each candidate comes from a tool-using agent working up to an hour in
  a fresh sandbox. Proposers see only a public score; a private split decides. The
  harness is an editable genome, changed only through a kernel-enforced gate with
  held-out and cost checks. Autonomous mode: an agent picks a problem and writes the

  reported. Single author, no seeds. On-front for `hce-evaluation`,
  `programmable-evaluator-oracle` and `information-firewall`.

### 16. Lessons from Luck: Do LLM-Written Lessons Carry Chance into an Agent's Next Decision?
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33029
- **Type:** paper (arXiv v1 2026-10-12, announced in window; registered protocol, code)
- **Authors:** Nakayashiki (1)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** Registered study: 256 synthetic worlds, 11 endpoints, 90,084 answers.
  After a chance failure of the better tool, lessons carried the chance: the next run
  chose the worse tool 11.6% of the time, against 0.4% with an irrelevant item
  (interaction +0.112 [+0.097, +0.126]). The effect is +0.042 when the log states that
  failures are independent, +0.181 when it doesn't. A decision-quality note in the
  writing instruction lowers the rate to 7.5%, and a no-change option to 3.1%; neither
  met the registered harm-reduction rule at every endpoint.
- **Why:** Noise entering memory: the write-side twin of the noise band. On-front
  for `verified-memory-writes` and `skill-library-lifecycle`. Same author as
  `nakayashiki2026when`.

### 17. Position, Role or Label? A Controlled Study of Instruction Conflicts in LLM Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33201
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-23; code and data)
- **Authors:** Laddha, Pradhan, Srivastava (3)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** WhoWins: 1,080 code-scored scenarios with up to five mutually
  exclusive instructions, and seven experiments that vary only delivery. 14 open-weight

  Relevant to where CLAUDE.md, rules and skills land in the stack. Open-weight only.

### 19. When Skills Tell Agents Not to Ask: A Large-Scale Study of Oversight Directives in Agent Skills
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37734
- **Type:** paper (arXiv, 2026-10-27)
- **Authors:** Sanko, Tian (2)
- **Relevance:** 4
- **Summary:** 1.53M English skills from GitSkills; 2,097 hand-labelled units plus a
  trained detector. About 1 in 17 skills (~88k) contains an oversight-loosening

  loosening is a useful lens on `agency.md`. On-front for `shared-skill-namespace`

### 20. rTaste: Do Research Agents Have Taste? Evaluating Early Stage Research Judgment
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40488

- **Authors:** Kanke (1)
- **Relevance:** 4
